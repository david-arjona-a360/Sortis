INTERACTIVE OFFICE MAP - INSTALLATION
=====================================

1. Run Interactive_Office_Map_Setup_v2.1.0.exe
   (no administrator rights are needed).
2. The app installs to %LOCALAPPDATA%\SORTIS and launches at the end of
   the install. Shortcuts are added to the Start Menu (and optionally the
   desktop).

FIRST LAUNCH
------------
On the first run the app shows a "Setup Requirements" message. The app
reads its data from your OneDrive folder:

    OneDrive - a360inc\PTY Files - Documents\FLOOR PLAN

Make sure:

    1. OneDrive is installed, running and signed in with your a360inc account.
    2. The shared folder above exists in your OneDrive.
    3. Files are available locally, not cloud-only (right-click the folder
       in OneDrive -> "Always keep on this device").

If the required folders are missing, the app shows a "Configuration Error"
message and closes. Fix your OneDrive setup and launch the app again.
